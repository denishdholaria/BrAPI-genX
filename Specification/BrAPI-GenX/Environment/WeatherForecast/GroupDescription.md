# Group WeatherForecast

**Status:** BrAPI-GenX **STRUCTURAL_DRAFT** candidate under Request for Comments (RFC) 031; not an independently validated environmental data product or official Breeding Application Programming Interface (BrAPI) version 2.1 resource.

This group proposes registered, namespaced environmental metadata with immutable revisions and read/list operations. Its replay and changed-body conflict contract remains candidate behavior, not evidence of a deployed persistent scientific service. The draft requires namespaced `weatherForecastDbId`, issue time `forecastedAt`, `validFrom`, `validTo`, a `measurements` array, and `status`. Measurement entries require variable identifier and numeric value, but the array may be empty; there is no schema-level validity-window ordering rule, calibrated uncertainty, model run identity, or typed WeatherObservation link (D-90–D-92).

**Boundaries:** A weather forecast is a predictive product rather than a measured WeatherObservation. Its required issue and validity timestamps are not sufficient to prove forecast lead time, source authority or a statistically evaluated uncertainty model. The draft contains no typed WeatherObservation relation (D-91). A named Location, station, variable, scientific interval or data source does not itself prove an authenticated observation, reproducible model computation, accepted uncertainty, correct units or verified environmental provenance. Advisory endpoint neighbors are not typed source-data lineage. Outstanding decision D-91 and associated domain requirements remain explicitly unresolved.
