# Group Emissions

**Status:** BrAPI-GenX **STRUCTURAL_DRAFT** candidate under Request for Comments (RFC) 076. `Emissions` is a proposed reporting-metadata record, not a calculated greenhouse-gas inventory, certified carbon accounting result, authoritative emissions factor, verified climate benefit or assurance of any emissions reduction.

The request requires **only** `emissionsDbId`; the response additionally requires lifecycle `status`. `siteDbId`, reporting `period`, `greenhouseGas`, numeric `quantity`, `unit`, `supersedes`, and `provenance` are all optional. The period is a free-form string with no schema-enforced date, interval or calendar format. `greenhouseGas` and `unit` are unstructured strings with no vocabulary or unit compatibility constraints. The example `CO2e` (carbon dioxide equivalent) describes a **derived equivalence category**, not a distinct greenhouse-gas molecule. There is no required gas species or global-warming-potential assessment version, factor methodology, baseline, activity data, reporting boundary, accounting scope, uncertainty, evidence attachment, or source-pool specification. The schema can admit a record containing no measured emissions.

**Authority and interpretation:** The optional `siteDbId` is not an authenticated BrAPI Location relationship; there is **no `carbonStockDbId`** property despite the prior RFC's claimed link to GenX CarbonStock. Carbon-stock quantities are not interchangeable with emission flows or carbon dioxide equivalent; no conversion, independent verification or comparability across years and reporting scopes is defined. Official BrAPI Observations may convey recorded quantities, but this candidate is not an official Observation.

**Deferred decisions:** D-226 asks whether `siteDbId` should be required; D-227 asks whether `quantity` and `unit` should be required; D-228 asks whether greenhouse-gas terminology should be controlled. All remain unapproved.
