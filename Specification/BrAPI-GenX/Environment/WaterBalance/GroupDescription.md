# Group WaterBalance

**Status:** BrAPI-GenX **STRUCTURAL_DRAFT** candidate under Request for Comments (RFC) 068; not an independently validated environmental data product or official Breeding Application Programming Interface (BrAPI) version 2.1 resource.

This group proposes registered, namespaced environmental metadata with immutable revisions and read/list operations. Its replay and changed-body conflict contract remains candidate behavior, not evidence of a deployed persistent scientific service. Only `waterBalanceDbId` and lifecycle `status` are required; both `periodStart` and `periodEnd`, study/location references, six millimetre-labelled components and provenance are optional. The draft has no required accounting interval or enforcement of closure, sign convention, component consistency, source observation provenance, or typed WeatherObservation/IrrigationOperation/CropCycle link (D-202–D-204).

**Boundaries:** Official BrAPI version 2.1 provides Locations and observations, not an independently governed water-balance closure model. This GenX candidate records optional precipitation, irrigation, evapotranspiration, drainage, runoff and storage-change quantities. It does not check the residual, ensure a common interval, or validate source measurements. A water-balance record does not confer water rights or irrigation advice. A named Location, station, variable, scientific interval or data source does not itself prove an authenticated observation, reproducible model computation, accepted uncertainty, correct units or verified environmental provenance. Advisory endpoint neighbors are not typed source-data lineage. Outstanding decision D-204 and associated domain requirements remain explicitly unresolved.
