# Group DoubledHaploid

**Status:** BrAPI-GenX **STRUCTURAL_DRAFT** candidate under Request for Comments (RFC) 020; a program-record descriptor, not evidence of a verified doubled-haploid lineage, cytological doubling or independent conformance.

**Current contract:** Registration requires `doubledHaploidProgramDbId`, raw string `programDbId`, and free-text `method`; response adds lifecycle `status` (`active`, `completed`, `superseded`). `donorGermplasmDbId`, `haploidCount`, `doubledCount`, `startedAt`, `completedAt` and `provenance` are optional. Although the counts are integers, they have no nonnegative minimum, conditional requiredness or documented denominator/verification method. The `completed` status does not require completion time, counts, ploidy evidence or derived Germplasm identities. `method` does not separate haploid induction from chromosome doubling or specify a controlled procedure.

**Official overlap:** Official Breeding Application Programming Interface (BrAPI) version 2.1 Cross requests already permit `crossType: DOUBLE_HAPLOID`, with parents represented by CrossParent. This is not a complete doubled-haploid *program* record, but the draft must not imply that BrAPI cannot express the related crossing type. A program descriptor is not an actual Cross, validated ploidy assay, successful induction, regeneration event or new germplasm accession. Raw Program/donor strings are not confirmed authoritative cross-server joins.

**Scientific and governance gaps:** No explicit generation/plant identity, verified haploid/diploid status, genomic validation, sampling definition, induction denominator, doubling treatment details, population linkage or proven relationship to official Cross is specified. D-57 (Program requiredness), D-58 (separate chromosome-doubling method), and D-59 (counts at completed state) remain open; no biological outcome or approval is inferred.
