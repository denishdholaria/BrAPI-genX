# Group SelectionDecisions

**Status:** BrAPI-GenX **STRUCTURAL_DRAFT** candidate under Request for Comments (RFC) 008. Its detailed authority-qualified design remains non-normative and does not demonstrate a permission-checked, evidence-supported breeding decision service.

**Contract boundary:** The request and response require `selectionDecisionDbId`, `programReference`, enumerated `decision` (`selected`, `rejected`, `deferred`, `advanced`), and date-formatted `decisionDate`. `candidateGermplasmReference`, `studyReference`, `populationReference`, `decisionMaker`, `selectionCriterion`, `rationale`, `linkedObservationReferences`, `status` and `provenance` are optional. A valid declared verdict may therefore omit candidate, responsible actor, scientific evidence and rationale. A date of recording does not establish meeting time, authorization, informed approval or a completed crossing.

**Authority and process:** `programReference` uses `BrapiResourceReference` (generation, resource type, authoritative server and database ID); optional `pipelineContext` and `schemeContext` use exact version identities, with conditional compatibility rules described in RFC-008. These candidate structures support provenance-aware interpretation but do not by themselves verify external server ownership, access rights or referenced evidence. Scheme TaskDefinition defines a process; a SelectionDecision is an asserted outcome, not that design-time task.

**Overlap:** GenX ParentCandidateSelection separately requires a Program and Germplasm candidate as strings plus Boolean `selected`, but omits `decisionDate`. The two drafts have no typed relationship or automatic equivalence; `false` is not a lossless mapping to the SelectionDecision verdict enumeration. Official BrAPI Program, Germplasm, Observation, PlannedCross and Cross retain their own identities and authority. No selected record implies release, commercial approval, performed pollination or certified breeding gain.

**Open governance:** RFC-008-Q1 (program vs trial/study scope), Q2 (mandatory evidence), and Q3 (supersession authority) remain open. Without a required `decisionMaker`, this contract does not enforce original-decision-maker-only supersession. No maturity promotion or automatic merging of the two verdict representations is authorized.
