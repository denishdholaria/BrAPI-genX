# Group ParentCandidateSelection

**Status:** BrAPI-GenX **STRUCTURAL_DRAFT** candidate under Request for Comments (RFC) 028, not a normative parent-selection decision, verified cross-parent identity or evidence of decision-making authority.

**Contract boundary:** Registration requires `parentCandidateSelectionDbId`, `programDbId`, `candidateGermplasmDbId` and Boolean `selected`; the response additionally requires lifecycle `status`. `selectionCriterion`, `rationale` and `provenance` are optional. Program and candidate IDs are raw strings, not authority-qualified references with an explicit server or independently demonstrated joins. No decision date, decision-maker identity, evidence Observation reference, parent sex/role or actual/planned cross is modelled. `selected: false` does not distinguish rejected, deferred and other outcomes.

**Overlap and breeding provenance:** The separate GenX `SelectionDecision` draft already proposes a richer verdict (`selected`, `rejected`, `deferred`, `advanced`) with date and authority-qualified Program reference. Its candidate Germplasm reference is optional. These records cannot be assumed equal: `ParentCandidateSelection` is parent-specific and Boolean, whereas `SelectionDecision` covers broader advancement and different authority requirements. Neither schema contains a typed link to the other. Official BrAPI version 2.1 PlannedCross and Cross already represent their respective parent material/roles; a parent *candidate* selection does not create either record or prove a crossing event.

**Deferred decisions:** D-81 asks about Boolean requiredness (currently required); D-82 about criterion terminology (free text); D-83 about a link to SelectionDecision (**no such property currently exists**, rather than an optional typed link). Reconciliation of overlapping verdicts, authority, consent, scope and historical linkage requires explicit governance; no automated synchronization is approved.
