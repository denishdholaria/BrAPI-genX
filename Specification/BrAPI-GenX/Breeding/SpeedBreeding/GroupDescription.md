# Group SpeedBreeding

**Status:** BrAPI-GenX **STRUCTURAL_DRAFT** candidate under Request for Comments (RFC) 022; descriptive protocol registry, not proof of an executed controlled-environment experiment or an independently reproduced generation-time improvement.

**Current contract:** Registration requires `speedBreedingProtocolDbId`, raw string `programDbId`, and `protocolName`; response adds `status` (`active`, `deprecated`, `superseded`). `lightRegime`, `temperatureRegime`, numeric `photoperiod` (described as hours), integer `generationTime` (described as days), `description` and `provenance` are optional. The numeric fields have no minimum, maximum, or biological consistency constraints. Free-text regime fields do not encode a time-resolved schedule, photon flux, spectral distribution, temperature series, humidity, crop or growth-stage applicability, replication, or validation. A protocol name does not establish method reproducibility.

**Official overlap:** Official BrAPI version 2.1 Study already contains `environmentParameters`, `growthFacility`, `culturalPractices` and `experimentalDesign` metadata. Phenotyping Events represent recorded interventions over time. The GenX draft is an optional protocol descriptor, not a replacement for Study environmental context, instrument measurements, actual Events or measured development/flowering. No typed `studyDbId`, `breedingPipelineDbId`, generation-advancement result or independent trial evidence exists here.

**Deferred decisions:** D-63 (photoperiod/generation time requiredness), D-64 (BreedingPipeline linkage — **not present**, rather than already optional), and D-65 (structured generation-time quantity) remain unratified. Do not interpret a described 60-day generation time as a demonstrated treatment effect, validated protocol or approved operational schedule.
