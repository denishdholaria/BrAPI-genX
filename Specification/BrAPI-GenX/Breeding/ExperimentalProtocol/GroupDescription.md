# Group ExperimentalProtocol

**Status:** BrAPI-GenX **STRUCTURAL_DRAFT** candidate under Request for Comments (RFC) 117, not an authorized scientific protocol, validated procedure or independent service conformance result.

**Current contract:** Registration requires only `experimentalProtocolDbId`; the response also requires lifecycle `status`. `protocolName`, `protocolType`, `version`, `designType`, `treatments`, `replicates`, `randomization`, `blocks` and `provenance` are optional. `treatments`, `replicates` and `blocks` are strings, not structured treatment lists or checked numeric counts. No required procedural steps, timed interventions, doses/units, statistical method, validation evidence or approval exists.

**Official overlap and ownership:** BrAPI version 2.1 Study already carries experimental-design and cultural-practices metadata; Observation Units contain applied treatment factors; Events describe actual interventions, affected units and dates. Those different facts must not be conflated with a planned protocol. GenX `ProtocolLibrary` is a separate **draft** with no typed relationship to this record; operation-neighbor links to GenomicDataset do not establish provenance or derivation.

**Deferred questions:** No `studyDbId`, `experimentalDesignDbId`, `protocolLibraryDbId` or `genomicDatasetDbId` property is modelled. D-349 (whether `protocolName` is required), D-350 (controlled terminology) and D-351 (possible GenomicDataset link) remain unratified. Registration does not certify scientific validity or execution.
