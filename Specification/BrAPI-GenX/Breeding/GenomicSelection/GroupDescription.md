# Group GenomicSelection

**Status:** BrAPI-GenX **STRUCTURAL_DRAFT** candidate under Request for Comments (RFC) 055. This proposed genomic selection result records candidate metadata; it is not an independently authorized selection verdict, scientific validation of a prediction model, or an official BrAPI v2.1 resource.

The response schema requires `genomicSelectionDbId`, `analysisMethodDbId`, and lifecycle `status`; the registration request requires only `genomicSelectionDbId` and `analysisMethodDbId`. `selectedGermplasm`, `selectionThreshold`, `studyDbId`, `jobDbId`, and `provenance` are optional. `selectedGermplasm` is an optional array of strings, without validated germplasm ownership, stable population scope, ranking, trait weights, or explicit selection-decision authorization. `selectionThreshold` is an optional number without enforced measurement scale, boundary, index definition, or cross-context interpretation.

**Scientific and decision boundary:** A recorded list and threshold cannot establish that an approved breeder applied a defined selection policy, that the cited genotype predictions were valid, or that material was officially advanced or rejected. The separate GenX `SelectionDecision` schema models an actual verdict with required `decision`, `decisionDate`, and `programReference` fields; GenomicSelection does not substitute for that decision, and no equivalence or automatic projection is authorized. The required `analysisMethodDbId` is only a string, not a verified exact method version. No `genomicDatasetDbId` is modelled; D-163–D-165 remain open. Proposed immutable replay and lifecycle semantics do not prove persistent-service conformance.
