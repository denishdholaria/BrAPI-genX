# Group ProtocolLibrary

**Status:** BrAPI-GenX **STRUCTURAL_DRAFT** candidate under Request for Comments (RFC) 119; not proof of independent research asset validity, reproducibility, scholarly acceptance or enforced access permissions.

The proposed `ProtocolLibrary` record defines an independent namespaced identifier, lifecycle `status` and optional descriptive metadata, `supersedes` and provenance. Draft operations register, list and retrieve metadata. Their replay and immutable-publication descriptions are candidate rules, not verified persistent service behavior.

**Scientific evidence, archival and access boundaries:** The optional methods and description are free text and do not provide executable steps, sample-preparation parameters, controlled instruments, environmental conditions, method identifiers, input/output contracts or versioned document checksum. A library label is not an independently repeatable experimental protocol. Decisions beginning at D-355, together with the other open questions in the governing RFC, remain unresolved. The presence of a record and advisory operation links does not create a typed scientific correspondence, immutable bytes, rights grant or editorial approval.
