Four source-backed Germplasm group descriptions distinguish proposed record metadata from verified conservation outcomes.

- StorageLocation: optional temperature/humidity/count snapshots, no seed-lot placement linkage, verified monitoring, calibration or alarm history.
- ViabilityTest: test date and SeedLot identifier required; sample size, germination, viability percentage and method optional. No normal-seedling count, replicate protocol or standardised validation. Viability and germination are distinct concepts.
- RegenerationRecord: required method and date do not establish successful seed-lot multiplication, genetic purity or inventory movement.
- SafetyDuplication: a named backup site and date do not prove delivery or acceptance by a recipient; no typed storage location, custody or quantity assurance.
